# Researchers

The people and organizations listed here have made substantial public contributions to red teaming, OT/ICS security, physical security research, or adjacent fields. Following their work is one of the fastest ways to stay current with the field.

---

## Red Teaming and Adversary Simulation

- [Joe Vest](https://twitter.com/joevest) — Author of *Red Team Development and Operations* and co-founder of the Red Team Journal. His work on structuring and maturing red team programs is foundational reading.
- [Chris Truncer](https://twitter.com/christruncer) — Contributor to several open-source red team tools including Veil-Framework. Useful for understanding post-exploitation tradecraft in a research context.
- [Raphael Mudge](https://www.cobaltstrike.com/blog) — Creator of Cobalt Strike and author of extensive documentation on adversary simulation concepts. His archived blog posts are widely used in red team training.
- [Will Schroeder (@harmj0y)](https://harmj0y.medium.com/) — Core contributor to BloodHound and PowerSploit. Research on Active Directory attack paths has influenced how organizations approach identity security.

---

## OT/ICS Security

- [Ralph Langner](https://www.langner.com/) — Independent ICS security researcher known for the first public technical analysis of Stuxnet. His writing on ICS-specific threat modeling is still relevant.
- [Sean McBride](https://claroty.com/team82/) — Part of Claroty's Team82 research group, which publishes detailed ICS and IoT vulnerability research.
- [Dale Peterson](https://dale-peterson.com/) — Founder of Digital Bond and the S4 conference. Long-standing voice in ICS security with a focus on critical infrastructure risk.
- [Marina Krotofil](https://www.marinaratings.com/) — Researcher specializing in process control system attacks, particularly in how cyberattacks can manipulate industrial processes with physical consequences.
- [CISA ICS-CERT](https://www.cisa.gov/ics) — Official US government source for ICS vulnerability advisories, incident response guidance, and recommended practices. Every ICS practitioner should monitor their advisories.

## Physical Security Research

- [Deviant Ollam](https://www.deviantollam.com/) — Physical penetration tester and speaker known for practical work on locks, access control, and physical red teaming. Regularly presents at DEF CON and other major conferences.
- [LockPickingLawyer (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/c/lockpickinglawyer) — Produces detailed lock analysis videos that serve as practical references for understanding lock security ratings and vulnerabilities.
- [Han Fey](https://toool.nl/) — Dutch locksport researcher and TOOOL contributor with published work on bump key vulnerabilities and impressioning techniques.

---

## Security Research Organizations and Labs

- [Dragos](https://www.dragos.com/blog/) — ICS-focused threat intelligence firm. Their public threat reports on adversary groups targeting industrial environments are among the most detailed available.
- [Claroty Team82](https://claroty.com/team82/research) — Publishes regular vulnerability research on OT, IoT, and building management systems. Technical write-ups are well-documented and reproducible.
- [Mandiant (Google)](https://www.mandiant.com/resources) — Incident response firm with extensive published research on threat actor TTPs, including ICS-targeting groups like SANDWORM.
- [SANS ICS](https://www.sans.org/ics/) — Publishes practitioner-focused ICS security content, curriculum, and posters. The SANS ICS curriculum is a respected credential path.
- [Idaho National Laboratory (INL)](https://inl.gov/cybersecurity/) — National lab conducting ICS and critical infrastructure cybersecurity research. Publishes open reports on control system risk and resilience.

## Notable Authors and Publishers

- [Kim Zetter](https://www.wired.com/author/kim-zetter/) — Investigative journalist and author of *Countdown to Zero Day*, the definitive account of Stuxnet. Essential reading for understanding real-world ICS attacks.
- [Andy Greenberg](https://www.wired.com/author/andy-greenberg/) — *Wired* journalist and author of *Sandworm*, which covers the most destructive cyberattacks in history. Bridges technical and policy perspectives.
- [The MITRE Corporation](https://attack.mitre.org/) — Developers of the ATT&CK framework and ATT&CK for ICS. Essential reference for mapping adversary behavior to detection and defense strategies.
